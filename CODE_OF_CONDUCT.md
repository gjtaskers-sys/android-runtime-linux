# Code of Conduct

We want participation in Android Runtime to be constructive, respectful, and
focused on making the project safer and more reliable.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and welcoming to people with different experience
  levels and backgrounds.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and technical reasoning.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair their effects.
- Protect private information; do not publish someone else's personal details
  or credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized abuse, personal attacks,
doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without
consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the
maintainer's GitHub contact options. Include a description of the behavior and
links or other context when safe to do so. The maintainer will review reports
as fairly and confidentially as practical and may edit or remove content, issue
a warning, or restrict participation. Reports made in good faith should not
result in retaliation.

This code applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and other
spaces used for project coordination.
